# User cases

User flows in the current project, reviewed on 15 November 2026. Shared flows apply wherever the same control appears. Conditional flows require the relevant account, service, or feature to be enabled.

## Startup and shared interactions

- UI-01. Launch the app. Wait through font and database preparation, which names its current stage and, after ten seconds, says preparing local data is taking longer than usual, then enter the requested screen. A database failure replaces database-dependent screens with an error message, Try again and optional details.
- UI-02. Open the root URL. First-time users enter onboarding, returning users enter Home, and a failed onboarding-status lookup also opens Home.
- UI-03. Leave and reopen the app. Restore saved language, theme and other preferences and any signed-in session, then load local study data. A failed profile lookup shows a neutral Profile name, never part of the email address.
- UI-04. Lose connectivity. The offline banner appears at once when the device reports no connection, or after three seconds when the internet is confirmed unreachable, while local study remains available. Reconnecting removes the banner after a short delay and lets configured account sync resume unless it is paused (UI-14).
- UI-05. Sign in, reconnect, or return to the foreground. Background sync shows syncing, synced, offline, or error status. Successful sync refreshes affected data. A first account restore that would replace local study records or pending changes waits for consent (UI-14).
- UI-06. Open a shared sheet or dialog. It animates above the current screen, fits the viewport, and blocks background scrolling on web without shifting the page. On web, focus moves into it. Supported forms move above the software keyboard.
- UI-07. Dismiss a shared overlay through its close control, backdrop, web Escape, or native Back when allowed. On Android, Back closes the keyboard first. Return to the underlying screen and restore web focus. Escape closes the top shared overlay or menu first.
- UI-08. Open a confirmation. Cancel returns without applying the action. Confirm starts the action, disables duplicate confirmation, and prevents dismissal while the operation is pending.
- UI-09. Open a shared card-actions menu. Choose an enabled item to close the menu and run it, or dismiss outside it. On web, arrow keys, Home, End, Enter, Space and Escape work inside the menu without moving the reader or page beneath it. The menu moves above its trigger when space below is insufficient.
- UI-10. Receive temporary action feedback. A toast appears, then disappears after a time based on its length, up to six seconds. Achievement announcements use their own explicit dismissal flow.
- UI-11. Encounter a caught screen-render error. The fallback replaces that section or screen with Try Again, Home on a full screen, and a Report a problem link, while navigation and dismissal keep working. Select Try Again to render it again.
- UI-12. Resize or rotate the viewport. Screens, lists, navigation, and overlays use their phone, tablet, or desktop arrangements while keeping the current route.
- UI-13. Close an editor with unsaved changes (a note, reflection, comment, profile edit, deck settings or Journey response) by close, Cancel, Back, Escape, the backdrop or switching target. A confirmation offers Keep editing or Discard; Discard closes and clears only that editor. A failed save keeps the text. Signing out or switching account clears account-bound drafts at once without asking.
- UI-14. Sign in to an account with saved progress while this device holds local study records or pending changes. A confirmation names the account, lists the affected study categories and pending changes, and says account progress will replace them, reading stays available and this is not a merge or a backup. Use account progress applies it; Cancel sync keeps every local record and pauses sync. Sync paused with Resume sync then stays visible in the app and in Settings > Account, survives a restart, and never resumes by itself on reconnect, return to the app or sign-in. Resume sync prepares the request again with fresh counts. If local data changed after the request was prepared, it expired after five minutes, or applying it failed, the dialog says so and offers Prepare again. One account's pause or approval never applies to another account.
- UI-15. As a guest, see one optional account promotion on Home, and on Progress after a first completed review. It says signing in is optional sync, not a backup, and local progress stays on this device. Dismiss it with its labeled 44px close control: one dismissal hides the promotion on both screens for fourteen days on this device, after which it can return.

## Navigation and Home

- NAV-01. On a phone-width screen, use the four-tab bar to move between Home, Mushaf, Progress, and Settings. The selected destination changes its active appearance. Board and Journey are reached from entry rows (NAV-06).
- NAV-02. On a tablet-width screen, and on Mushaf at any width from 768px, open the navigation panel from its edge trigger by click, tap or keyboard. Hovering alone does not open it. The panel lists every destination and Search. Pressing outside only closes it and never activates what is underneath; Close, Escape, Android Back or choosing a destination also close it.
- NAV-03. On a desktop-width screen, use the persistent sidebar, which stays beside the page without covering it. It lists Home, Mushaf, Board, Progress, the reflection feed, the Reflection Journey, Settings and Search, and links to own profile. Wide Mushaf reading hides the sidebar and uses the edge panel (NAV-02).
- NAV-04. Open `/flashcards` or `/search` directly. Both replace themselves with Home: `/flashcards` scrolls to the deck cards and `/search?q=` opens Search with that query. Search and deck review start through Home's controls.
- NAV-05. Open an unknown URL. Read the not-found message and follow its Home link through the normal root-entry flow.
- NAV-06. On Home and Progress, use the labeled Board row to reach the Board, and on Progress the Reflection Journey row with its completed count (Home has the Journey card). Each opens its own screen with its current sign-in and opt-in rules, and Back returns to the row.
- HOME-01. Enter Home. View today's due count with its due and new split, memorized pages or ayat, the wird streak card and today's hifz plan, then Continue reading, the decks, and lower down journey progress with its current level (Journey complete once every level is done), the Board row and the guest account promotion. Placeholders hold the numbers and deck rows until the first load. When nothing is left in any deck or the plan, Home says all caught up. Returning from study, synced records, or resuming on a new local day refreshes the data; a load failure keeps the last values and Retry shows progress until it succeeds.
- HOME-02. Select Continue reading. Open Mushaf at the saved ayah as one explicit target. Without a saved position, a start card opens Al-Fatiha.
- HOME-03. As a guest, once sign-in has settled and account sync is configured, use Home's account promotion (UI-15) to open Login or Signup, which return to Home after sign-in.
- HOME-04. Dismiss an achievement announcement. Mark that achievement as seen and show the next unseen announcement when one exists. Newer unlocks wait behind the visible one with a "+N more" chip. Select the announcement to open the achievements list.
- HOME-05. Select the dashboard's Start review when cards are due, or select a deck with cards due today. Open the review session for all eligible cards or that deck. A deck with only future cards says nothing is due and opens its card list. Decks without cards appear under More decks and say no cards are in scope.
- HOME-06. Open a deck's configuration or card-list control without starting its review. The vocabulary deck appears only after vocabulary cards exist.
- HOME-07. Select the reflection-journey card. Open the journey and continue with its saved level and response state.

## Search and external Quran links

- SEARCH-01. Open search from Home, Mushaf, the sidebar Search entry, or on web Ctrl/Cmd+K or / outside text fields. Close any word tooltip/detail, focus the search field, and show recent searches or the initial hint.
- SEARCH-02. Enter at least two normalized characters in Text mode. Search Arabic Quran text and English translation after the typing delay, or submit immediately with the keyboard. Show results grouped by surah with each ayah's page and every match highlighted in a short snippet. A full page of 200 results says it shows the first 200. A loading placeholder appears only after 200 ms.
- SEARCH-03. Switch to Root mode with the same query. Search a root, lemma, full word, or partial word and show the matched root. When the word matches several roots, choose one or all. Show occurrences grouped by form, with every group open when there are 30 places or fewer, otherwise the first. Expand or collapse groups.
- SEARCH-04. Select a search result. Close search and the keyboard, then open or reposition Mushaf at the ayah as one explicit target. An occurrence result also identifies and highlights the matching word. Typing a reference such as 2:255, a surah name or number, page 62 or ص ٦٢ offers Go to for that ayah, surah or page's first ayah; a shared surah name lists each surah, and an out-of-range reference explains the valid range.
- SEARCH-05. Pressing the search key or opening a result saves the query and mode to the front of the ten-entry history; typing alone does not. Select a recent search to restore its query and mode, close the keyboard and rerun it. Remove one recent search with its X.
- SEARCH-06. Clear the query with the field's X to return to history, or clear all history. Cancel closes search. Closing search clears its query, results, errors, and expanded groups while retaining the selected search mode, and ignores any search still running.
- SEARCH-07. Enter fewer than two normalized characters without running a query, with a hint to type at least two letters. A completed query with no matches names the query and offers Try root search for a single Arabic word in Text mode or Search as text in Root mode, keeping the query. A failed query shows Retry for the current query.
- SEARCH-08. Follow `/open?surah=2&ayah=255` or the equivalent app link. Briefly show the link bridge, then replace it with Mushaf at the requested ayah. The pending target takes priority over the saved position. A valid link opens reading even before onboarding is complete, and the next ordinary launch still follows the onboarding decision.
- SEARCH-09. Follow an open link whose surah or ayah is missing, malformed or outside the Quran, such as surah 2 ayah 999. Arabic-Indic digits are accepted. A localized message says the link does not open an ayah and offers Home and Search. The saved reading position stays as it was, and the link is never clamped to a nearby ayah. Surahs run from 1 to 114 and ayahs follow each surah's real length.

## Onboarding

- START-01. Begin onboarding from Welcome, where a language pill switches to the other interface language. Get Started opens the hifz setup; Skip for now saves onboarding completion and enters Home. A completion-save failure keeps Welcome open with an error.
- START-02. In setup, choose a goal: memorize, keep what is memorized, or both. Continue through what is memorized, review tracks, pace, and when to review. Back returns one step, and Not now skips an optional step with its defaults.
- START-03. Choose what is memorized by juz (Juz 30 and 29 first) or by surah with name or number search. An empty selection is valid. Surah rows show localized names and ayah counts; Select results adds only the matching surahs, no matches offer Clear search, and a failed surah list offers Retry. The summary counts and names the selection. Saving declares those pages memorized and spreads their first checks over the first cycle.
- START-04. Save the setup once. Back stays disabled while the save runs. Save onboarding completion and open the first session at once: page review when pages were declared, sabaq when new memorization is planned, the ayah review in ayah mode, or Home otherwise. A save failure keeps the last step and choices open with an error for another Save; a completion failure after the plan saved offers Retry without saving the plan again.
- START-05. Open onboarding again after completing it on a restored account. Completion is synced, so a restored account with completed onboarding enters Home.

## Mushaf reading and movement

- READ-01. Open Mushaf in the saved verse/page mode. Restore a matching saved position when available, otherwise begin at the initial position. Deliberate scrolling or paging updates the saved reading location; arriving at a jump target does not replace it until you read on.
- READ-02. Switch from page view to verse view at the current page's first ayah. Switch back to the page containing the top visible ayah.
- READ-03. Read continuous vertical pages, or choose Swipe in the Page layout group of the More menu (beside the mode label on wide screens) to turn horizontal pages. Scroll returns to vertical movement. Choosing closes the menu and changes page movement.
- READ-04. Turn horizontal pages by touch or pen swipe, or by horizontal wheel/trackpad movement. A mouse drag selects Quran text instead of turning the page. Short gestures return to the current page. The first and last pages bound movement.
- READ-05. On web, press Space or PageDown for the next page and Shift+Space or PageUp for the previous one. In page view, ArrowLeft advances and ArrowRight goes back, in either UI language. Text fields, open sheets and menus, and a focused control keep their own keys.
- READ-06. Drag, scroll, or wheel the page rail to preview a page, then release or settle to commit the jump. The preview names the page and juz, and longer ticks mark each juz start. The rail is available in normal page reading.
- READ-07. Open Go to from More, the page rail's expand control, or the surah and juz labels above the verses. Choose Surah or Juz, filter by number or Arabic/English name, and select an entry to close navigation and jump. Typing a reference such as 2:255 or p 300 (also Arabic-Indic digits) adds a row for that ayah or page. Switching tabs clears the filter. Empty matches and data-load failures have their own states.
- READ-08. Open a surah header's information panel. Read its localized information or loading/unavailable state, then return to the reader.
- READ-09. Scroll down to hide reader controls and up to reveal them. On phone/tablet, tap a non-control reading area to toggle the controls. Text selection and control presses avoid this toggle.
- READ-10. Arrive from search, bookmarks, notes, progress, reflections, or a word occurrence. Scroll to the ayah and optional word and show a soft highlight behind it. The highlight clears after three seconds or a deliberate tap, not when the page scrolls slightly.
- READ-11. Use Home from the reader's More menu (or the Home button on a wide toolbar). Leave Mushaf and open Home with the latest reading position available for resumption.
- READ-12. Wait for Quran data or page fonts. Render reading content when ready. A failed Quran data load shows a message with Retry that loads it again.
- READ-13. Press ? outside text fields and open sheets, or choose Shortcuts and gestures in the reader's More menu. A help sheet explains the ayah-number and word gestures and, on web, the keyboard: Space and PageDown, PageUp and Shift+Space, the arrow keys in page view, Ctrl/Cmd+K or / to open Search, ? for the list, and Escape. Ctrl/Cmd+K and / also open Search from other screens, except while typing in a field.
- READ-14. After an explicit jump (a search result, bookmark, Go to, a link or a word occurrence) with a previous reading position, a Back to where I was pill appears for five seconds. Select it to return to that position and view once. A later jump replaces it, leaving the reader or sending the app to the background dismisses it, and another account never uses it.
- READ-15. Use the reader toolbar. Below 768px it has six 44px buttons: the view switch, Search, Hide/Show, Recitation, Reading settings and More, wrapping onto a second row on very narrow screens instead of shrinking. Each has a name shown on hover, focus or long press. The view switch names the current view and the switch action. More holds Bookmarks, Go to, Home, Focus when available, Shortcuts and gestures and, in page view, the Page layout choices. From 768px, when the full toolbar fits, the view switch shows a short label, page view adds a direction button, and Bookmarks, Go to, Home and Focus get their own buttons.

## Memorization and focus

- HIFZ-01. In verse view, hide ayahs and tap a hidden ayah's placeholder, sized like the ayah, to reveal it. Re-hide it only with its Hide this ayah action. Reveals survive scrolling within the current page and same-page jumps; scrolling or jumping to another page keeps only that page's reveals. Turning hiding off clears every reveal. Nothing here changes Hifz records.
- HIFZ-02. In page view, turn on page reveal mode. Hide words and replace the page rail with memorization controls that stay visible when the toolbar hides. Reveal the next word or the next ayah, or hide the last revealed ayah. Boundary controls disable when no action remains.
- HIFZ-03. Start automatic word revelation: the first word appears at once, then one word per interval shown in seconds. Adjust the interval from the rail or the Auto reveal section of reading settings, then stop it. Manual reveal/hide stops automatic progress.
- HIFZ-04. Reveal a whole page. See Page revealed with Repeat and Next page (no Next page on 604), or advance automatically when the saved auto-advance preference permits it. Moving to another page resets revelation; same-page jumps and view switches keep it.
- FOCUS-01. Enter focus mode from vertical page view while Hifz is off. Hide normal navigation with scrolling paused until Play. While playing, the screen stays on and idle controls shrink to one Pause pill after 2.5 seconds. Pause/resume (Space on web), adjust speed with its time per page, show the focus controls, or exit (Escape on web) to normal reading.
- FOCUS-02. Scroll, navigate, open ayah actions/word detail, or open a reading panel during focus mode. Pause automatic movement and show Paused. Reaching the end pauses with a three-second notice; incompatible reading settings exit focus mode.

## Ayah actions and details

- AYAH-01. Press an ayah reference in verse view, or long-press a page ayah marker. Open the ayah-action panel.
- AYAH-02. Use the action panel to copy the ayah, add/remove its bookmark, choose/change a highlight color, or remove an existing highlight. Successful actions close the panel and show feedback; pending actions prevent repeated writes.
- AYAH-03. From the same panel, choose reflection or private note. Open its editor above the action panel for the selected ayah. Closing the editor closes both panels.
- AYAH-04. Tap an ayah marker in page view, on every platform, or a content pill in verse view, to open ayah detail. A marker opens Tafsir and a pill selects its corresponding tab. A long press on a marker selects the ayah (AYAH-01) and does not also open detail.
- AYAH-05. In detail, move to the previous/next ayah with its arrows (or the arrow keys when not typing), keeping the selected tab. Disable movement at Quran boundaries. Stepping never moves the reader underneath; Go to this ayah closes detail and moves the reader there.
- AYAH-06. Switch detail tabs, in this order: Similar verses (when the ayah is similar to a memorized one), Translation, Tafsir, Notes, Qira'at (when the ayah has qira'at) and Reflections (when the service is configured). Translation stays available in Arabic UI whenever a non-Arabic translation source is selected. A tab that does not apply to the next ayah falls back to Tafsir. The row scrolls sideways and fades where more tabs wait.
- AYAH-07. Read the selected translation in its text direction, or its loading placeholder. Read tafsir, switch among available sources, or open Manage sources and use the shared source-selection flow.
- AYAH-08. Read qira'at for the ayah and any covered-ayah group. The tab appears when the ayah has qira'at, or when checking failed, in which case it shows an error instead of claiming none exist. Loading and missing-content states replace the text when needed.
- AYAH-10. From ayah detail, copy the ayah with its reference and link, use Share where the device supports it, toggle its bookmark, or use enabled audio playback. On a verse card the share icon opens the system share sheet, or on web without sharing copies to the clipboard and is labeled Copy. Copy, share and bookmark failures show feedback, and a dismissed share sheet is not reported as success. Report an issue with this ayah opens a GitHub issue form prefilled with the ayah reference, the active tab and source, and the app version, platform and language only; if it cannot open, a dialog shows the link to copy.
- AYAH-11. Open the Similar verses tab of an ayah that is similar to one already memorized. Each memorized similar verse shows with this one, with the shared wording marked and the verses' meanings: the translation, or in Arabic the Muyassar tafsir. Moving to an ayah without one returns to Tafsir. The tab is hidden while Mark similar verses is off.

## Words and browser text selection

- WORD-01. On web, tap or click a visible word to show its Arabic/English meaning tooltip at once, in page and verse view alike. A drag, text selection or long press cancels it. Press it again to dismiss, select another word to replace it, or scroll/press elsewhere to close it. Press the tooltip to open word detail.
- WORD-02. Long-press a visible word or right-click it on web to open detail. Horizontal touch-web pages retain browser long-press text selection. Hidden words ignore word actions; native does not show the floating tooltip.
- WORD-03. In word detail, browse previous/next words and view their reference and frequency. The root opens its occurrences, and Arabic UI also shows the lemma. Switch Meaning, Grammar (I'rab), Tajweed, Morphology (Tasreef) and Occurrences tabs. Boundary arrows disable; closing resets the initial tab to Meaning.
- WORD-04. Open the current word's ayah detail above the word sheet. Meaning starts Translation where available, otherwise Tafsir. Other word tabs start Tafsir. Close ayah detail to return to the word sheet.
- WORD-05. Read the word translation/transliteration or Arabic meaning. Add its meaning to vocabulary, then show saving/saved feedback or a retryable failure.
- WORD-06. When Arabic meaning is absent, enter a custom meaning and save it locally. The saved meaning gains the normal vocabulary action. Empty text cannot save; a failure retains the editor and error. Your own meaning offers Edit and a confirmed Delete; a bundled meaning offers neither.
- WORD-07. Read I'rab with the selected word highlighted and scrolled into view, or read Tasreef morphology. Loading/missing-data states replace unavailable content.
- WORD-08. Expand or collapse individual Tajweed rules in the selected UI language. Show the no-rules state when none applies.
- WORD-09. Select a root occurrence. Close word detail and move Mushaf to that ayah/word with a highlight. Missing roots or occurrences show an empty state.
- SELECT-01. On web, select Quran text with mouse, keyboard, or touch. Copy through the custom menu or normal clipboard command to obtain Unicode Quran text and references.
- SELECT-02. Open the selected-text color picker and highlight the exact selected words. Show copy/highlight errors when an operation fails. Scrolling or outside interaction dismisses the selection menu; active browser selection suppresses page dragging.
- SAVED-01. Open bookmarks from the reader toolbar. Order them Recent or Quran order and, when there are more than ten, search them by surah name or number. Select one to close the sheet and navigate to its ayah, or remove one with its trash control after a confirmation that names it. The sheet stays open. Show an empty list, no matches, deletion progress, or success/failure feedback as applicable; a failed removal keeps the row and the dialog.

## Recitation

- AUDIO-01. Play a single ayah from verse view or detail. The player bar shows the surah and ayah, reciter and loading, playing, paused or failed state, with pause/resume, stop and the recitation sheet. Select another ayah to replace playback. Single-ayah playback cancels a playing range. Loading blocks repeated requests and gives up after 15 seconds with Retry; a late answer never starts. Offline, an unavailable recitation and a playback failure read differently; recitation needs an internet connection.
- AUDIO-02. Open recitation settings. Start with the visible ayah, choose one ayah, an inclusive start/end range, or the current surah, then select a reciter through the shared reciter picker.
- AUDIO-03. Set whole-range repeats, repeats per ayah, and delay; they are kept on this device. Start playback: the sheet closes and the player bar shows the surah, ayah and progress, dimmed with Paused while paused. Reopen the sheet from the bar to see repeat counters, then stop or let it finish.
- AUDIO-04. Submit a malformed reference (Arabic-Indic digits are accepted), nonexistent single-ayah reference, reversed/empty range, or failed playback request. Keep the recitation sheet open with the message under the field that caused it. Unavailable audio also reports failure.
- AUDIO-05. Background the native app or lock the screen during playback. Recitation keeps playing with lock-screen controls titled with the surah name and ayah, the reciter and Hafiz. Pausing there, or a platform pause such as unplugged headphones where the platform supports it, shows as paused on return; returning restores the actual player state and current ayah without a new play command.
- AUDIO-06. With Follow recitation on (the default), a range recitation keeps its current ayah in view. Scroll, page or jump away: following stops for that recitation without stopping audio and one Back to ayah control appears. Use it to recenter and follow again. Turn Follow recitation off in the recitation sheet to stop following; single ayahs are never followed.

## Deck setup and card management

- DECK-01. Configure a built-in retention, mutashabihat, similar-tails, qiraat, or reasons-of-revelation deck. Choose all Quran, selected surahs, or selected juz numbers.
- DECK-02. Add/remove surahs and edit optional start/end ayah ranges in nested selectors. Return with Done to the parent draft. Empty scope, noninteger/out-of-bounds ranges, or reversed ranges prevent Save.
- DECK-03. In built-in deck configuration or vocabulary review settings, adjust daily and new-card limits (whole numbers, with an inline message when too low), target retention, maximum interval, fuzz, short-term scheduling, and learning/relearning steps, with the advanced options grouped. Steppers enforce their configured bounds.
- DECK-04. Enter scheduling steps with supported minute/hour/day units, including Arabic units. A preview reads Will use followed by the parsed steps, or no steps when the field is blank. Unparseable text shows a hint such as 1m, 10m, 1d and keeps Save disabled.
- DECK-05. Set reviews first, new cards first, or mixed order. Sort reviews by due date, oldest, newest, or random order, and new cards by creation or random order.
- DECK-06. In vocabulary settings, enable Arabic and/or translated-meaning answers while keeping at least one enabled. Open a setting's explanation and dismiss it to return to the same draft.
- DECK-07. Save built-in configuration or vocabulary settings. Save changes, show a notice naming the deck, refresh Home, and close. Built-in configuration also prepares eligible cards. Loading/save failures show an error and keep the draft. Closing with unsaved changes asks to discard (UI-13).
- DECK-08. Open a deck's card list. Read card references, state, and localized due dates with a count of the cards shown; narrow screens stack each row, and Show more loads further cards. Loading, empty, and failed-load states are distinct.
- DECK-09. Combine card text search with one filter: All, Due, Scheduled, New, Learning, Review, Relearning, Marked, Suspended, or Buried, grouped as states and flags. Show matching rows or no matches. Closing resets search/filter state.
- DECK-10. Open a card menu and open its ayah in Mushaf, mark/unmark, suspend/unsuspend, or bury/unbury it. Save the change and refresh list/Home counts, or show action failure.
- DECK-11. Choose Reset progress or Delete from a card menu. A confirmation names the card; cancel or confirm. Confirmed actions reset/remove the card and refresh counts. Generated virtual cards cannot be deleted.
- DECK-12. Set a card's due date. Edit and save, or cancel. Reject invalid or past dates, then refresh the list/counts after a valid save.

## Review sessions

- REVIEW-01. Start a normal review from Home or onboarding. Load eligible cards using deck scope, scheduling limits, and ordering. An empty/exhausted queue opens the zero-card summary; a load failure shows an error with Try again and a way out.
- REVIEW-02. Read the card front and reveal its answer. Step through available answer sides, then grade. Missing sides are skipped; a card with no available answers goes directly to grading.
- REVIEW-03. Review an ayah or vocabulary card. Answer sides can contain next/previous ayah, translation, tafsir, surah name, or enabled Arabic/translated word meanings. Vocabulary cards highlight the word in its ayah context.
- REVIEW-04. Review a specialty card. Reveal matching-ayah comparisons, similar endings, qiraat variants, or revelation context/source according to its smart-deck type.
- REVIEW-05. Choose Again, Hard, Good, or Easy with the displayed interval, once every answer side has been shown. On web, Space or Enter reveals and steps through sides and 1 to 4 choose Again, Hard, Good and Easy whichever way the screen is laid out; typing, open sheets and held keys are ignored. Save scheduling/activity, then advance. Cards due again today can return later in the same session. A save failure keeps the current card with an error and Try again, and one retry records one review.
- REVIEW-06. Open current-card actions and mark/unmark the card. Return to the same card. Suspend or bury instead to remove it from the queue and advance or finish.
- REVIEW-07. Delete or reset the current card through confirmation. Cancel leaves it unchanged. Delete advances; reset returns the same card to its front with reset progress.
- REVIEW-08. Edit the current/first available answer mode. Save personal nonblank answer text, save blank text to restore its default, or cancel and return to the card.
- REVIEW-09. Set a valid due date of today or later for the current card. Save and remove it from this session, then advance or finish. Invalid input stays in the editor.
- REVIEW-10. Press X, or Android Back, during an active session. With no grade saved yet, return to Home. After a grade, confirm leaving first; Stay returns to the card. Already saved grades and card changes remain.
- REVIEW-11. Finish the queue. Read reviewed/new/review/relearning totals, duration, and next review, then the wird cards (WIRD-08). Choose Done, or "I'll be back tomorrow" during the first week, to return Home.
- REVIEW-12. Open `/flashcards/vocab` directly, for example from an old link. The route immediately replaces itself with the main review session for the Vocabulary Review deck, before anything is loaded, so that deck's settings, grades, card actions and states apply (REVIEW-01 to REVIEW-11). Back from the session does not return to the old URL. Older saved-word rows are left unchanged.

## Wird days and streak

- WIRD-01. View the Home streak card. It shows the streak length and today's state: no wird yet, done today, excused, paused, at risk, yesterday still open to make up, an earn-back in progress, or a fresh start. Progress toward today's minimum shows until it is met.
- WIRD-02. Grade reviews until today's minimum wird is met: one page of recitation or five minutes of graded review by default. The review that meets it marks the day done once and shows a notice. Study cards count only when meanings count toward the wird.
- WIRD-03. Miss a day and recite before Dhuhr the next day, or 12:30 without prayer times. Every review graded then counts toward making up yesterday until it reaches the minimum, which makes yesterday up; later reviews count for today. Reviews of a make-up that is not finished in time stay with it and do not count for today. On any device the same reviews count for the same day. If another device with an earlier cutoff already covered yesterday with a grace day, finishing the make-up before this device's cutoff still makes yesterday up and gives the grace day back.
- WIRD-04. Miss a day after the make-up window closes while holding a grace day. A grace day covers it automatically, and Home mentions it the next morning. Grace days are earned after 3 wird days, then every 7, holding at most 2.
- WIRD-05. Open Excuse or pause on Home and mark excused days from 3 days back to 30 ahead with a reason. Excused days keep the streak without counting toward it. Withdraw an excuse from the same sheet; a recited day keeps its status.
- WIRD-06. Pause the streak for up to 60 days, then end the pause early from the same sheet. Paused days neither break nor extend the streak.

## Hifz plan and recitation

- PLAN-01. View today's plan card on Home: pages left with their kind and minutes, progress, Start review, Memorize new lines when sabaq is planned, and spots to strengthen with Drill now. A complete plan says so.
- PLAN-02. Start the wird review. Recite each planned page at a support level: hidden, swipe to reveal, line by line, first letters, or full text. The level starts from the page's recall, and full text is practice only.
- PLAN-03. While reciting, Peek reveals the next word, Show me reveals the rest of the line, and Slip counts a self-corrected slip. Done reciting reveals the page for checking.
- PLAN-04. Check the page. Tap a word to mark a mistake by type, change or remove it. When the next page is also memorized, check the continuation into it as clean or stumbled. The rubric proposes a grade, and the support level caps it.
- PLAN-05. Grade the page. Save the grade, errors, and support level in one step and move to the next page. Recited in salah grades the page Good without a check. With a teacher or partner listening, the grade records them as the grader.
- PLAN-06. Grade a page Again with every error in one half. That half returns in 10 minutes and the next day. A stumble into the next page brings back its seam the same way. Both appear after the pages and count as spots to strengthen.
- PLAN-07. Drill the mistake inbox from Home. Recite each noted ayah, check it, and mark it clean, still needing work, or not a mistake, which removes it. An item clears after clean recalls on two later days and expires after 28 days.
- PLAN-08. Memorize new lines in sabaq. Set an intention, listen, read the meaning and any similar verses already memorized, each with its translation or, in Arabic, its Muyassar tafsir, look over the lines, then recite each ayah, the chunk, and its link to the lines before it from memory, and grade the check. The lines are saved once the chunk is recited cleanly the set number of times in a row, so leaving before the check keeps them. The chunk joins the daily review. When a halaqa teacher has set the next sabaq, the lesson takes the next of those lines first, at the plan's daily pace, and says the teacher assigned them; once they are memorized, the plan's order goes on. Offline, the last assignment this account saw applies.
- PLAN-09. Return after 7 or more days away. Home offers a welcome-back review that starts with the strongest pages at a chosen support level.
- PLAN-10. Accept or decline the monthly check on Home. It reviews 5 memorized pages not seen in two weeks and reports how many were clean.
- PLAN-11. View hifz progress: memorized pages, how many are steady, fair, or need review, completed juz, and steady weeks.
- PLAN-12. Leave a hifz review after grading a page. Confirm leaving first, then read the summary with pages, clean pages, spots marked, half pages and seams, minutes, and the wird cards.
- PLAN-13. Miss the same word in 3 or more sessions. It becomes a weak spot: the Mushaf marks it with a small dot (a dotted underline in verse view on some devices) unless turned off, and a fill-the-gap card shows its ayah with that word hidden until Check, then asks whether it was recited cleanly. Mistakes from other devices count, including after signing in on a new device. Dismissing a mistake on any device removes the card once the word is wrong in fewer than 3 sessions.
- PLAN-14. Drift into a similar verse while reciting a page. Mark the word as a drift: the next session adds the page of the verse it drifted into, and a "Which one?" prompt shows both verses with their shared wording marked until one is chosen. Verses that read the same also show the end of the ayah before them, or the start of the ayah after. Once one is chosen, each verse shows its translation, or the Muyassar tafsir in Arabic. The prompt returns in the next 3 sessions. Marking the drift as not a mistake removes its page and prompt from the session, unless another drift still needs the page.
- PLAN-15. Review a page that holds a similar verse whose sibling is memorized. Two pages later, or at the end when fewer are left, the wird review asks "Which one?" for that pair. Each group is asked about once a session, at most 3 times in all, and not when a drift prompt already asks about it. The prompt is not graded and does not count toward the wird.
- PLAN-16. Read an ayah in the Mushaf that is similar to one already memorized. Page view puts a small dot on its ayah number, and verse view shows a similar-verses button beside its bookmark. Tapping the mark opens the ayah's Similar verses tab. A long press on a marked ayah number still selects the ayah. Mark similar verses in reader settings or General turns the marks off. A page whose similar verses cannot be read shows no marks.
